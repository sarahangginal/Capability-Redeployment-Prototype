Executive overview

Start with future work. Then find the capabilities that can move into it.

The prototype models the redeployment mechanism: future-work demand → capability requirements → current workforce evidence → capability gap → human-gated action.
Case trigger

~4,200 roles' worth of work is changing in Field Metering & Manual Operations.

The prototype does not assume that those are 4,200 people to remove. It asks what work remains, what capabilities that work needs, and which sampled employees have evidence of those capabilities.

How to read the signals

Coverage = demonstrated capability against assessed requirements. Evidence coverage = how much of the requirement has usable evidence. Confidence = quality of that evidence. Missing remains unknown.
